Principles

Built to stay out of your way.

Most tools compete for your attention. Stillpoint is designed to need as little of it as possible, so your team's week stays yours.

No dashboards

Nothing new to check. Stillpoint works quietly inside the calendar your team already uses.

No notifications by default

One short summary a week. Anything more is opt-in and easy to switch off.

Suggests, never cancels

Nothing on your calendar changes until you say yes. You stay in charge of your team's time.

Your calendar stays yours

Encrypted, never sold and never used to train AI models. Disconnect and it's deleted.
